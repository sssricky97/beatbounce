Hugging Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Hugging Pop ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player arcade game published by Ricky Laser Art. This policy explains how the Game handles your information.

Summary

Hugging Pop does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. The Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. It works fully offline.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Advertising ID
  • Usage analytics or crash data
  • Any data sent to a server (the Game makes no network requests)

Information Stored On Your Device Only

To make the Game playable, the following is saved locally on your device using standard app local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party:

  • Your best height and best combo score
  • Your settings (sound on/off, music on/off, selected mode and difficulty)
  • Whether you have seen the intro/tutorial

This data is removed if you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, the Game complies with COPPA and the Google Play Families policy.

Permissions

The Game requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.

Third-Party Content

The Game uses the open-source Phaser game engine (MIT License) and the Fredoka and Caveat fonts (SIL Open Font License). These are bundled locally and do not collect any data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
